# V3 Design

Mobile-first consumer-app UI pass based on the agreed Zomato + Urban Company + Uber benchmark.

- Primary navigation: Home, Workers, Reports, Payments
- Secondary navigation: This month, History, Household under More
- Larger touch targets and clearer active states
- Cleaner cards, forms, reports and settlement surfaces
- Existing authentication, Firebase data model, multi-user access and shift-aware calculations remain unchanged

This branch is a candidate for review only. Do not treat it as production until the PR is reviewed and merged.
